Who this is for

The Chief Operating Officer of a U.S. regional bank with roughly $20–60 billion in assets. Her board has just asked for a three-year digital plan, and directors keep holding up DBS as the model. She has to show what to build first, who decides the fights that will follow, and how the board will know by the second QBR whether it's working.

What she gets

  • Roadmap: See which foundation each pilot depends on, so if the data or cloud work slips she knows exactly which pilot to defer (the dependency table and the 'remove one' test).
  • RACI: Walk into the first steering committee with one named owner for each of seven recurring fights. That includes who freezes releases after an outage (D6), the decision DBS had to have imposed from outside.
  • Dashboard: Copy a QBR layout built from DBS's own disclosed numbers at its halfway point, including the digital-vs-traditional cost-income split her CFO will ask for.
  • Week 5 tool: Score her own bank first with our Group 2 regional-bank maturity tool, then use this roadmap to decide where to start.

What we added beyond the brief

Two of us work in banking, so we read DBS the way a bank COO would, not as a tech story. Three choices show it. First, we put Customer Journey Discipline in Crawl even though it isn't technology, because digibank's paperless, signatureless onboarding is a redesigned journey, not a digitized form (Roadmap, dependency 2). Second, we set the dashboard in Q4 2017, the quarter DBS first published digital-versus-traditional economics. That way every current value is a number DBS reported, and our own targets are marked team-set (Dashboard, M1, M5–M7). That choice also exposed a real sequencing risk: Car Marketplace went live three months before the API portal (Roadmap, reality check). Third, we added a decision DBS didn't have written down: who trades release speed for resilience after an outage (RACI, D6). Its faster releases came with outages in 2021–2023, and MAS imposed a six-month change freeze in November 2023. Each RACI row also names what enforces it, because a decision right nobody enforces isn't real.
