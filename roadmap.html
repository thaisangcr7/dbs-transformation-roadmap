"Remove one" test (6.41): Take out GANDALF Cloud & DevOps Core and digibank India, the API platform and ADA & AI Industrialisation all break. Take out Technology Insourcing and nobody in-house can build and run 155 APIs at DevOps speed. Take out Customer Journey Discipline and digibank becomes a digital copy of a paper form, and the scorecard has no digital behaviours to measure.

Crawl — why it has to come before the next phase

Crawl comes first because every later initiative runs on it. GANDALF Cloud & DevOps Core gave DBS cloud-ready apps and roughly 10x faster releases. Technology Insourcing gave it engineers who own the code. Customer Journey Discipline taught senior managers to redesign work from the customer back. None of the three needed another to start, so they ran in parallel. Pull any one out and a Walk item fails: without the cloud there's no phone-only bank, without in-house engineers there are no 155 APIs, and without journey thinking there's no paperless onboarding.

Walk — why it has to come before the next phase

Walk tests the foundation in one market or segment before betting the bank. digibank went live in India only, the API platform opened in Singapore only, and the Digital Value Capture Scorecard covered just consumer and SME customers in Singapore and Hong Kong. Each produced a number that justified the next step: 1.6 million digibank customers in about 19 months, 50+ partners live at launch, and a 34% digital cost-income ratio against 55% for traditional customers. Run had to wait for these results, because they were the evidence used to fund scale.

Run — why it has to come before the next phase

Run scales what Walk proved, and it needs organizational change, not just more technology. Ecosystem Marketplaces took the partner APIs into car, electricity, property and travel journeys, which only works once partners can plug in safely. ADA & AI Industrialisation took the AI first proven in digibank's assistant to 2,000+ models. That needed the 2018 Platform Operating Model, where 33 business-and-technology 'two-in-a-box' teams share KPIs and funding, so every AI use case has an owner. Starting here in 2014 would have meant AI on vendor-run systems and siloed data.

Reality check: where DBS doesn't fit the textbook

Crawl ran about four years, not 6–12 months. GANDALF began in 2014, and the cloud move wasn't finished until 2018–2019. At bank scale, a better Crawl marker is the first usable increment: 100% of apps on DevOps by end-2017.

One Run item started early. Car Marketplace went live in August 2017 (after a December 2016 pilot), three months before the API portal's public launch. The APIs were already being built through 2017, and the scale-out to electricity, property and travel came after launch. We flag it on the dashboard as a sequencing risk.

Gaps in the public record. DBS doesn't publish exact start dates for GANDALF or ADA, so those quarter ranges are our best reading of the sources.
